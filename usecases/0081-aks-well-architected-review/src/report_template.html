      How to read the local index

      Local index = 100 × passed checks / evaluated checks. Coverage compares evaluated checks with evaluated plus not evaluated checks. Not applicable checks are excluded from both denominators.

      The engine identifies each check by a unique check_id and never counts it twice in the overall index when you browse namespaces. This view shows the indexes it receives; it does not re-evaluate controls or generate recommendations.

      This is not an official Azure Assessment, Azure Well-Architected, WARA or Polaris score. The references guide the review; they do not prove compliance, absence of risk, an SLA or a root cause.
